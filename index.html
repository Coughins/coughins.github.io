Mermaid Live for Confluence

Render Mermaid diagrams-as-code on Confluence Cloud pages.

  • Current. Ships Mermaid 11.17.2, and stays current with upstream releases.
  • Live preview. The editor renders as you type, with syntax errors reported by line number.
  • Private by construction. Diagrams render in your browser. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.
  • Fast. A 7.6 KB shell loads first; the renderer and each diagram type load only when needed.

Status: in review for the Atlassian Marketplace.

Documentation

Adding a diagram

On a Confluence page, type /Mermaid and choose Mermaid Diagram. The editor opens automatically. Write or paste Mermaid syntax, check the live preview, then Save and publish the page.

Supported diagram types

Everything supported by Mermaid 11, including flowcharts, sequence diagrams, state diagrams, class diagrams, entity-relationship diagrams, Gantt charts, pie charts, mindmaps, and timelines. Full syntax reference: mermaid.js.org.

Working with AI-generated diagrams

Mermaid is the syntax most AI assistants produce when asked for a diagram. Paste the block straight into the editor — the live preview tells you immediately whether it is valid.

Themes

Diagrams follow your Confluence theme and re-render when you switch between light and dark.

Known limitations

  • On PDF export, Confluence renders the diagram but may clip tall diagrams at the bottom. We are working on this.

Privacy policy

We collect nothing. That is not a policy choice we could quietly reverse — it is a property of how the app is built.

Personal data collectedNone.
Page content accessedNone. The app declares no Atlassian data access scopes.
Data sent to usNone. The app makes no external network requests.
Analytics or trackingNone. No cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts.
Data shared or soldNone. There is nothing to share.

Your diagram source is stored by Atlassian as ordinary macro configuration on your own Confluence page, inside your own Atlassian site, exactly like any other page content. We never receive it. Diagram rendering happens locally in your browser.

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, rectify, or erase on request. Any data subject request concerning your Confluence content is handled by Atlassian as your data processor. See Atlassian's privacy policy.
